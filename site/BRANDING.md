# Sophia branding

Daniel selected the Lady Wisdom and open-book mark created for the DC Labs
GitHub profile on 30 October 2026. It replaces the older green S.

Original source: `danielcuesta007/danielcuesta007`, commit
`64c10590b83a44dd4701be4542dd03a6f778130f`,
`icons/sophia-lady-wisdom.png`.

The website variant is the standalone olive Lady Wisdom and open-book silhouette
on a transparent background, without a square, tile, frame or cream margin. Pages use white surfaces and neutral grays.
`icon-512.png` is shared by headers, footers, feature and download sections;
`favicon.png` and `apple-touch-icon.png` derive from that same artwork.
